# Imaguncula Code of Conduct

## Standard

Imaguncula contributors and maintainers must make project spaces safe, respectful, and useful. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, commits, documentation, and project events.

Acceptable conduct includes:

- discussing technical decisions on their evidence and tradeoffs;
- giving specific, constructive feedback;
- respecting different backgrounds, viewpoints, and levels of experience;
- accepting responsibility and correcting mistakes;
- protecting private information and unpublished work.

Unacceptable conduct includes:

- harassment, threats, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention;
- discriminatory, insulting, or demeaning language or imagery;
- trolling, sustained disruption, or personal and political attacks;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional project.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in project spaces and when a person officially represents Imaguncula. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary to protect the project and its community.

Report conduct privately to the repository owner using a non-public contact method listed on the owner's GitHub profile. Do not disclose personal or sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers must protect the reporter's privacy, investigate reports fairly, and avoid conflicts of interest. A maintainer named in a report must not decide its outcome.
